# House rules

**Base system: D&D 5e (2014).** The books on this site are the 2014 ones. A few 2024 rules are
in play, listed here; anything not listed runs as written in 2014.

## From the 2024 rules

### Potions
- **Drinking a potion is a bonus action.** Giving one to a creature within 5 feet is also a
  bonus action. (2014 made both an action.)
- Potion of Healing amounts use the 2024 numbers:

| Potion | Heals |
| --- | --- |
| Potion of Healing (common) | 2d4 + 2 |
| Greater | 4d4 + 4 |
| Superior | 8d4 + 8 |
| Supreme | 10d4 + 20 |

## Table rulings

Rulings made at the table get logged here so they stay consistent.

- Critical hits: 2014 (double the dice).
- Flanking: off.
- Encumbrance: off.
- Inspiration: granted by the DM for good play; one at a time.
